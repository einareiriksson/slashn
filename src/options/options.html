slashn

Omnibox: type s, space, then [system] <alias> [value]. s ? lists systems. The toolbar icon opens the same launcher as a popup.

Keys are what you type in the omnibox (lowercase). Exactly one system is the default; otherwise the most recently used one is picked. Host has no scheme or path, e.g. my123456.s4hana.cloud.sap.

Editing a seed or library row creates a personal copy that shadows it; deleting one hides it. Personal rows and hidden markers sync across your Chrome profile. Seed and library rows are refreshed on every extension update.

Unknown t-codes are also guessed from their prefix (ME2x → PurchaseOrder, VA4x → SalesContract…) and offered as guess when the target tenant actually has that intent. Low-confidence seed mappings disappear once a scan shows the tenant lacks the intent.
